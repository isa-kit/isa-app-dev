What this is

This is where features audition before they make it to the real site. Things here may be half-built, break mid-click, or quietly vanish overnight while someone rebuilds them. Nothing here is a promise.

What to expect

  • Data you put in here can be reset without warning — don't keep anything you'd miss.
  • The bugs are real bugs, not a demo of what bugs look like.
  • If it's broken, it's probably known. If it's not, tell us and it will be.

Where to actually go

The stable, finished site lives at devisant.com. The app running on this build is at /isa/ — go there to poke at what's currently under construction.
